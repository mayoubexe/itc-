Moon Pancakes 🌙

The easiest breakfast in the galaxy!

Ingredients

  • flour
  • egg
  • milk
  • sugar

How to make them

  1. mix all the Ingredients in a bowl
  2. pour a little mix into a warm pan
  3. cook both sides until golden
  4. add your favorite topping!

chef's tip: Your pancakes are already perfect. All that's left is to add your personal touch and enjoy them with the people you love.
